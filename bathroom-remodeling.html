A quieter, more considered bathroom.

Cutting Edge Design & Construction plans bathroom remodels that bring together tile, stone, vanities, lighting and comfortable daily use. The focus is a coordinated renovation, from the shower layout and preparation beneath the finishes to the final details.

What the project can include

  • Primary bathroom and guest bathroom renovations
  • Shower layout, tile installation and finish detailing
  • Vanities, stone surfaces, storage and lighting
  • Coordination of ventilation, plumbing and electrical requirements

What shapes the investment

Shower size, waterproofing requirements, layout changes, plumbing relocations, tile complexity, stone and fixtures all affect the investment. Existing conditions behind finishes can only be assessed properly as the scope is developed.

Pricing and timing are established for the individual project after its scope and existing conditions are reviewed.

Where we work

Prepare for the first conversation

Bring photographs, approximate dimensions, your city and the changes you want to make. Tell us whether this is the only bathroom available during construction and whether accessibility or storage is a priority.

Planning your project

Questions homeowners ask

Can the bathroom layout be changed?

Layout changes can be considered after reviewing the existing structure and plumbing. Feasibility and the effect on the scope are established before a proposal is finalized.

How is a bathroom project planned around an occupied home?

Access, work areas, protection and the availability of other bathrooms should be discussed during planning. The construction sequence depends on the scope and material availability.

Do you help coordinate tile, vanity and fixture selections?

Yes. The project can coordinate tile, stone, cabinetry, lighting and fixtures as one design so the finishes and practical details work together.

Tell us what you want to change.

Share the property location, rooms or outdoor areas involved and the result you have in mind.
