Planning your project

Questions homeowners ask

Can a renovation be completed in phases?

Phasing can be explored around access, occupied areas and the dependencies between trades. Some work is more practical to complete together; this is reviewed during planning.

What does a whole-home remodeling proposal depend on?

It depends on the existing property, the rooms included, the agreed design direction, finish selections and any required work to structure or building systems.

Can the project keep elements of the existing home?

Yes. The planning process can identify features to retain and develop new finishes and details around them, subject to their condition and the overall scope.
