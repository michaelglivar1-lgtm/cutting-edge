Luxury Bathroom Remodel in Jacksonville — Frequently Asked

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Jacksonville?

Bathroom remodels in Jacksonville typically run $18,000–$70,000. Spa-grade master baths with curbless showers, freestanding tubs, natural stone, and wellness features can exceed $100,000. We give a line-item estimate after a free consultation.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Most bathroom remodels take 3–6 weeks of construction. Custom tile, stone, and specialty fixtures can add lead time, which we plan into the schedule up front.

Can you make my bathroom a curbless / spa-style retreat?

Yes. Curbless walk-in showers, steam systems, heated floors, soaking tubs, and wellness features are a specialty. We handle the waterproofing, drainage engineering, and structural details these designs require.
