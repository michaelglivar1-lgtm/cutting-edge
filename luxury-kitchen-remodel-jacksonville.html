Luxury Kitchen Remodel in Jacksonville — Frequently Asked

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Jacksonville?

Most kitchen remodels in Jacksonville run $35,000–$120,000 depending on size, cabinetry level, and appliances. Luxury design-build kitchens with custom cabinetry, stone counters, and layout changes often exceed $120,000. We provide a detailed line-item estimate after a free on-site consultation.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

A typical kitchen remodel takes 6–12 weeks of construction once materials are on site. Custom cabinetry and stone can add 4–8 weeks of lead time, so we order early and phase the work to keep your home livable.

Do I need permits for a kitchen remodel in Florida?

Cosmetic updates usually don't need permits, but any electrical, plumbing, gas, or structural work does. As a Florida Certified General Contractor (CGC060801), we pull all required permits and schedule inspections for you.
