How it works

The clock, handled

Every exchange runs on IRS deadlines. Ours are met the same way we build — early, and on paper.

DAY 0

The relinquished property closes

Cash goes to your qualified intermediary. Call us now — the 45-day clock starts on closing day, and the best inventory gets matched before it's contested.

DAY 1–45

Identification window

We present off-market candidates matched to your buy-box, or structure a build-to-suit on land we source. Identification letters go to your QI with time to spare.

DAY 45–180

Close and build

Replacement property closes, then our crews execute the improvement exchange — draws coordinated with your intermediary, construction documented to exchange rules.

DAY 180+

You own finished, not vacant

The exchange completes with the improvement underway or complete — value built in, not parked.
