Water damage questions, answered

What should I do first when I find water damage?

If it's safe, stop the source — shut off the water main for a plumbing failure — then call us right away at (303) 434-7658. Take photos of everything before you move anything, and keep clear of standing water near electrical outlets or appliances. Mold can begin growing within 24 to 48 hours, so the clock matters.

Will my homeowner's insurance cover water damage restoration?

Often, yes — most policies cover sudden and accidental damage like burst pipes, appliance failures and storm-driven roof leaks. Gradual leaks from deferred maintenance are usually excluded. We document everything with photos and moisture readings to support your claim and coordinate with your adjuster.

How fast can you respond?

Fast. Active water intrusion is our top priority — call (303) 434-7658 now and we'll get a crew to your home as quickly as possible. Every hour of standing water means more damage to drywall, flooring and framing.

Do you handle mold?

Prevention first: proper extraction, drying and antimicrobial treatment stop mold before it starts. If mold is already established, we coordinate a licensed Florida mold remediator and then manage the full rebuild after clearance — you still deal with one contractor: us.

Do you rebuild after the dry-out?

Yes — that's the advantage of hiring a licensed general contractor instead of a dry-out-only company. Water extraction, structural drying and the complete rebuild — drywall, flooring, cabinets, paint — all under one contract with one accountable team.
