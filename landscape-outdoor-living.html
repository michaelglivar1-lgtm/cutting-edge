Planning your project

Questions homeowners ask

Can outdoor work be coordinated with an interior remodel?

Yes. Planning the projects together can help align access, material selections and the transition between indoor rooms and outdoor spaces.

What should be reviewed before a landscape or patio project?

The starting review includes access, existing conditions, intended use, utilities and any applicable local or community requirements. The final scope is specific to the property.

Do you provide a standard price for every outdoor project?

No. The site, materials and construction requirements vary. A project-specific assessment is needed to define the work and establish a proposal.
