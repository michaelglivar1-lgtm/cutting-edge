Dock Installation in Orlando — Frequently Asked

How much does a new dock cost in Orlando?

Residential docks in Orlando typically run $15,000–$90,000 depending on size, materials, and whether you add a boat lift. Large yacht docks with lifts and full utilities can exceed $150,000.

Do I need permits to build a dock in Florida?

Yes. Dock construction requires permits from your city/county, Florida DEP, and often the Army Corps of Engineers. We manage the full permitting and environmental process.

How long does dock construction take?

Most residential docks take 4–10 weeks of construction once permits are issued. Permitting itself can take 1–4 months depending on environmental review, so we start that early.
