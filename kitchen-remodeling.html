What the project can include

  • Layout planning and coordination of the kitchen work sequence
  • Cabinetry, storage details, countertops and backsplashes
  • Flooring, lighting and finish coordination
  • Coordination of plumbing and electrical work where the scope requires it

What shapes the investment

The main cost drivers are room size, changes to the layout, cabinetry construction, stone selection, appliance requirements and the condition of existing systems. Keeping plumbing locations can simplify some projects; moving walls or services requires a more detailed assessment.

Pricing and timing are established for the individual project after its scope and existing conditions are reviewed.

Planning your project

Questions homeowners ask

Can you coordinate a kitchen remodel with adjoining rooms?

Yes. A kitchen project can be planned as part of a wider interior renovation so flooring, lighting, finishes and transitions work together.

Can I get a firm price from photographs alone?

Photographs help start the conversation. A project-specific scope and assessment of existing conditions are needed before a firm proposal can be established.

Will every kitchen remodel need the same permits?

Permit requirements depend on the property and proposed work. Changes involving structure, electrical or plumbing systems should be reviewed for the applicable approvals before construction.
