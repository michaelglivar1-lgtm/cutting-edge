Phased Execution on Occupied Hotels — Protecting RevPAR

A PIP rarely allows for a full closure. Most owners need the property cash-flowing through the renovation, which makes sequencing the single largest driver of both budget and guest satisfaction. Cutting Edge Design & Construction builds PIP schedules around occupancy patterns, seasonal demand, and group booking windows so displaced room count stays within a threshold the property can absorb.

Our typical approach:

  • Floor-by-floor or wing-by-wing takedowns — we isolate active construction zones with hard partitions, negative-air containment, and dedicated service elevators to keep dust, noise, and trades away from sold rooms.
  • Off-hours corridor and lobby work — high-visibility public spaces (lobby, breakfast area, corridors, fitness, pool deck) are staged in phases and, where possible, executed during low-traffic windows to preserve first impressions and guest reviews.
  • Prefabricated and mock-up rooms first — we build a brand-approved model room early so the franchisor, owner, and design team sign off on finishes, casegoods fit, and installation quality before we roll the scope across the full inventory.
  • Weather-aware envelope and exterior scopes — across Florida and the coastal Southeast, hurricane season, humidity, and wind-borne debris requirements shape when we tackle roofing, windows, balconies, and porte-cochère work.

Brand Standards & PIP Compliance

A PIP is a contractual document between the owner and the franchisor — every line item maps to a brand standard, a deadline, and, often, a franchise agreement extension or key-money condition. Cutting Edge Design & Construction reads the PIP the way an asset manager does: as a schedule of obligations that must be closed out and documented, not just a punch list.

We work across Marriott, Hilton, IHG, Hyatt, Wyndham, and Choice brand families, aligning delivered scope with each brand's current design guidelines, approved vendor programs, and prototype specifications. That includes:

  • Coordinating FF&E procurement and soft-goods packages against brand-approved manufacturers and lead times.
  • Meeting life-safety and ADA requirements written into the PIP — sprinkler, alarm, egress, guest-room accessibility counts, and pool/spa compliance.
  • Documenting completed scope with photos, cut sheets, and sign-offs so the property clears its brand inspection the first time and avoids default notices.
  • Flagging PIP items that conflict with current code, existing conditions, or realistic lead times — and resolving them with the brand before they become schedule risk.

Cost Control & Procurement Timing

PIP budgets are typically fixed at acquisition or refinance, which leaves little room for surprise. The two most common budget killers we see are long-lead FF&E ordered too late and undiscovered building-system deficiencies behind the walls. Cutting Edge Design & Construction addresses both up front: we pre-order long-lead items against a locked model-room approval, and we run existing-conditions assessments — MEP, moisture, structural, and envelope — before demolition so change orders are identified early, not mid-project.

We give owners open-book pricing, milestone draw schedules that align with lender and franchisor requirements, and a hard completion date tied to the PIP deadline. That transparency is what asset managers and ownership groups managing multiple properties across the Eastern Seaboard rely on.

FAQ

Can Cutting Edge Design & Construction keep our hotel open during the PIP?
Yes. The majority of our PIP renovations are performed on occupied properties. We phase the work by floor or wing, contain construction zones, and sequence public-space and exterior work to keep displaced rooms within an occupancy threshold you set.

Do you handle FF&E procurement or just installation?
Both. Cutting Edge Design & Construction can manage FF&E and soft-goods procurement against brand-approved vendors and lead times, coordinate delivery and warehousing, and handle installation — or integrate with an owner-supplied purchasing agent if you already have one.

How do you make sure we pass the brand inspection?
We build and get sign-off on a model room before rolling out the full scope, execute to current brand design standards, and document every completed PIP line item with photos and cut sheets. That package is what clears your franchisor inspection and closes out the PIP.
