## True orientation (verified)

The north arrow on Sheet C-7 is plumb — **true north = top of sheet**. The stated site orientation is correct. One naming note: the road the civil drawings label **"State Road A1A"** along the north edge **is** the E. 3rd Ave / South Causeway approach — same road, state designation.

## Site layout as drawn (C-7)

- **Phase I — 4-story hotel**, 114 rooms, building height 55'-0", in the **southwest** of the parcel; long axis runs roughly **east–west**.
- **Existing ground-level pool** at the hotel's **SW corner**, hard against Callalisa Creek (this is the "rear mangrove-view pool" location).
- **Porte-cochère / main entry** on the hotel's **north** face, off the internal drive from E. 3rd Ave.
- **Phase II — restaurant** (Type D, outdoor seating, 8,550 sf, 278 seats) with parking in the **west-central** area.
- Large **surface parking** and future-commercial ground on the **east** side of the parcel.
- Dog-walk park along the creek (west); Dunkin Donuts outparcel at the NW near A1A.
- FFE 9.50 (hotel) / 9.00; Flood Zone AE (FIRM 12127C0544 J, 9/29/2017).

## Building design (BASE4 A3.1 / A3.2)

Coastal-Florida Hyatt Place prototype:
- Tan/"Macadamia" EIFS field walls; cream lap-siding and white-"Heron" trim bays.
- Blue-grey ("Belclaire Breeze") glazed storefront ground level.
- Coquina/"Coastal Reef" cut-stone base.
- Dark-bronze juliet-balcony railings and louvered shutters.
- Grey standing-seam metal gable roofs with exposed timber brackets.
- Central entry tower with gabled "HYATT PLACE" sign + cupola/weathervane; heavy timber porte-cochère on stone columns.
- South (rear) elevation is the balcony side; a ground-level timber pergola/trellis terrace faces the mangroves.
