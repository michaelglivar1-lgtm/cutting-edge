# Concept Render Prompts — refined to the real parcel orientation

Hyatt Place – New Smyrna Beach. All prompts below are calibrated to the **confirmed** site: E. 3rd Ave to the **north**, Indian River + causeway bridge to the **west**, Callalisa Creek mangroves along the **west/south**, Atlantic ~1 mile **east**. Sun **rises east over the Atlantic**, **sets west over the Indian River**. This orientation matters — it fixes where shadows fall, which façade catches golden hour, and which way the rooftop bar faces.

## Reusable blocks

**STYLE ANCHOR** (paste into every prompt):
> Photorealistic architectural concept render, Hyatt Place brand aesthetic: clean contemporary mid-rise, warm neutral stucco + composite panel façade, expansive floor-to-ceiling guestroom glazing, subtle horizontal banding, understated porte-cochère, tasteful landscape lighting. Coastal Florida palette — sand, driftwood gray, sea-glass accents. Golden-hour or blue-hour lighting, physically-based materials, ultra-detailed, 8k, professional real-estate photography, 24mm–35mm architectural lens, correct vertical perspective (no keystoning).

**PROGRAM (5-story concept):**
> 5 stories (concept adds a 5th floor to the approved 4), ~114+ guest rooms, ~72,000 sf, fully sprinklered R-1. Rooftop amenity deck with public pool + tiki bar & grill. A separate rear ground-level pool with mangrove views. A 4-level parking garage set inland on the north/east side.

**NEGATIVE / AVOID:**
> No high-rise skyscraper, no downtown density, no snow, no mountains, no distorted verticals, no oversaturated HDR halos, no visible brand-name signage errors, no people with distorted faces, no fantasy architecture. Keep it a believable 5-story coastal select-service hotel.

## 1 — Aerial hero (the money shot)

Camera **over the Indian River to the west/southwest, looking east/northeast** at the building. This puts the Callalisa Creek mangroves in the foreground, the hotel mid-frame catching low sun on its west façade, and the inland/Atlantic side beyond. The setting sun is *behind the camera*, lighting the front of the building — ideal.

> [STYLE ANCHOR] Drone aerial at golden hour, camera positioned over the Indian River southwest of the site looking east-northeast. Foreground: lush green Callalisa Creek mangrove wetlands and tidal creek. Midground: a 5-story Hyatt Place hotel [PROGRAM], its west and south façades warmly lit by the low western sun. Rooftop amenity deck visible up top with a pool and a tiki bar under a thatched/timber canopy. Behind and inland (north/east) sits a 4-level parking garage, lower and secondary to the hotel. The causeway bridge and Indian River glint in the near distance to the west. Long soft shadows cast eastward (inland). [AVOID]

## 2 — Arrival / street-level entry (from E. 3rd Ave, north)

Main entry and porte-cochère face **north toward E. 3rd Ave**. Morning or midday light (sun in the east/south) reads well on the north-facing entry as ambient/sky light.

> [STYLE ANCHOR] Eye-level architectural photo from E. 3rd Ave looking south at the hotel's main arrival. Contemporary Hyatt Place porte-cochère with warm downlighting, "Hyatt Place" brand signage, drop-off drive court, palms and coastal grasses in the median. 5-story guestroom tower rising behind the entry. Soft late-morning coastal light, clear Florida sky. A hint of the inland parking garage to the left (east). [AVOID]

## 3 — Rooftop pool + tiki bar & grill at sunset (faces WEST)

This is the signature amenity. The rooftop bar **must face west** — that's where the Indian River sunset is. Camera stands on the roof deck looking west over the railing.

> [STYLE ANCHOR] Twilight rooftop lifestyle render on the 5th-floor amenity deck, camera looking west over the pool toward the Indian River. Infinity-edge-feel public pool reflecting a pink-and-orange sunset sky, a tiki bar and grill under a timber-and-thatch canopy with string lights, lounge seating, fire feature, glass wind screens. The Indian River, the causeway bridge silhouette, and distant mangroves glow in the setting sun on the horizon. Warm bar lighting vs cool blue-hour sky. Relaxed coastal-luxe mood. [AVOID people-face distortion]

## 4 — Rear mangrove-view pool (ground level, west/south)

Ground-level pool tucked on the **west/south side** facing the Callalisa Creek mangroves — quiet, shaded, nature-forward. Softer, dappled light.

> [STYLE ANCHOR] Ground-level resort pool on the hotel's west/south side, camera looking outward toward dense green Callalisa Creek mangroves and tidal creek just beyond a low railing. Rectangular pool with sun shelf, cabanas and chaise lounges, native coastal landscaping, boardwalk edge. Dappled afternoon light filtering through mangrove canopy, calm and private atmosphere, subtle reflections. The 5-story hotel rises behind the camera (not in frame or only as soft shadow). [AVOID]

## 5 — Site/context aerial (shows the whole program + garage)

Higher, wider drone shot to communicate the site plan: hotel on the water (west/south) side, garage inland (north/east), access off E. 3rd Ave to the north.

> [STYLE ANCHOR] High wide drone shot looking down at ~45°, north roughly toward the top of frame. Bottom/left (west/south): Indian River, causeway bridge, and Callalisa Creek mangroves wrapping the site. The 5-story Hyatt Place hotel sits on the water side with its rooftop pool/tiki deck visible. Top/right (north/east, inland): a 4-level ~240ft x 240ft parking garage connected by a landscaped walkway. E. 3rd Ave / South Causeway runs along the north edge as the site entrance. Late-afternoon light, clear coastal Florida day, believable 9.5-acre PUD site plan. [AVOID]

## Orientation cheat-sheet for any new prompt
- Want the **setting sun / golden hour on the façade**? Shoot **from the west** (over the Indian River) looking east.
- Want the **sunset in the background** (bar, rooftop)? Face **west**.
- **Mangroves** belong on the **west and south** edges — never north or the inland east.
- **Garage** belongs **inland (north/east)** and should read as secondary/lower than the hotel.
- **Main entrance / arrival** is off **E. 3rd Ave to the north**.
- Morning light comes from the **east (Atlantic)**; evening from the **west (river)**.
