Seawall Construction in Tampa — Frequently Asked

How much does a new seawall cost in Tampa?

Most residential seawalls run $450–$1,200 per linear foot depending on water depth, wall height, and access. A typical 100-ft residential replacement often lands between $60,000 and $120,000 all-in.

Do I need permits to build or replace a seawall?

Yes. Nearly all seawall work in Florida requires permits from your city/county plus Florida DEP and often the Army Corps of Engineers. We handle all permitting and environmental review.

How long does a seawall last?

A well-built modern concrete or vinyl seawall lasts 40–60 years in Florida's marine environment. Warning signs for replacement include cracking panels, soil washout behind the cap, and rusting rebar.
